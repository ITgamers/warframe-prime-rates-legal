# Privacy Policy

**Warframe Prime Rates** · by steamonlyitgamer

**Effective:** September 12, 2026

## The short version

Warframe Prime Rates has no servers and no accounts of its own. We do not
receive, see or store your data. Everything the app keeps stays on your
computer.

The app does talk to other services on your behalf — warframe.market,
Digital Extremes and a few public reference sources — and it is distributed by
Overwolf, which serves the ads in the app. Those services handle data under
their own policies, described below.

We do not sell data, we do not run analytics of our own, and we do not track
you.

## What the app keeps on your computer

All of this lives in the app's folder on your PC. None of it is sent to us.

- **Market data** — price history and live orders for tradeable items.
- **Your inventory** — read from Warframe through Overwolf's game events
  service, so the app can price what you own.
- **Your trade history** — read from Warframe's own log file (`EE.log`). This
  includes the **in-game names of players you traded with**, the items and the
  platinum involved, because that is what the game records.
- **Your warframe.market listings, saved builds, plans and settings**
  (such as theme and zoom).
- **Cached artwork** for items and mastery ranks, downloaded from Digital
  Extremes and the Warframe wiki.
- **Window size and position.**
- **A support log**, capped at 1 MB, recording technical events such as the app
  starting or a connection failing. It does not contain your inventory or
  anyone's chat messages.

### Your warframe.market sign-in

If you sign in to warframe.market, your email and password are sent **directly
to warframe.market over an encrypted connection**. The app does not store your
password, and it never reaches us.

warframe.market returns a session token, which the app saves in a file that
only your Windows account can read, so you stay signed in. Signing out deletes
it.

If a sign-in fails, the app may save a short diagnostic file containing the
error codes and the first part of warframe.market's response. It never contains
your password or your token.

### Warframe's log and chat

The app reads Warframe's log file on your computer to detect trades and
incoming whispers. When whisper alerts are on, chat lines are checked to decide
whether to show you a notification and are then discarded — **they are not
saved**.

### Optional imports

If you choose to import an export file from another tool (such as AlecaFrame),
it is read from your computer and the trades in it are added to your local
history. Nothing from it leaves your computer.

## Services the app contacts

| Service | Why | What they receive |
|---|---|---|
| **warframe.market** | Prices, orders, your profile and messages, your online status, and sign-in | Your requests, and — when you are signed in — your account token. Listings and order changes you make are sent under your account. |
| **Digital Extremes** (`warframe.com` servers) | Public game data, the in-game world state, item artwork | Standard web request details such as your IP address |
| **Warframe wiki** and **GitHub** (community data) | Public reference data for builds and mastery rank artwork | Standard web request details |
| **PayPal** | Only if you choose to donate — it opens in your browser | Whatever you give PayPal directly |
| **Overwolf** | Distributes the app, provides game events, serves ads | See below |

Requests to warframe.market identify the app by name and version, as
warframe.market's rules require.

Each of these services processes data under its own privacy policy, not this
one.

## Overwolf and advertising

Warframe Prime Rates is distributed through **Overwolf**, and the ads in the
app are served by Overwolf's advertising platform. Overwolf and its advertising
partners may collect information such as device identifiers, usage data and
how you interact with ads, under **Overwolf's privacy policy**
([legal.overwolf.com](https://legal.overwolf.com)).

Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before any ad is
shown. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under
**Settings → Ad privacy → Manage**.

## Your choices

- **Sign out** of warframe.market in the app to delete your session token.
- **Change ad consent** under Settings → Ad privacy → Manage.
- **Delete your data** by removing the app's folder:
  `%APPDATA%\Warframe Prime Rates\`
- **Uninstall** the app at any time.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to export or delete
on your behalf — it is all already in your hands.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them.
